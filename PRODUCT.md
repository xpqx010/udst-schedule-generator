# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

UDST students planning an academic term from the section options shown in PeopleSoft. They need to enter the courses they intend to take, attach one or more screenshots to each course, verify the extracted options, and compare valid schedules without unrelated courses appearing in the result.

## Product Purpose

The product turns student-supplied course screenshots into conflict-free schedule options. Success means every result contains exactly one complete option for every course the student entered, preserves bundled lectures, laboratories, and tutorials, excludes closed options, and remains easy to verify before the student registers manually in PeopleSoft.

## Positioning

A precise course-planning tool that generates schedules only from the courses and screenshots supplied by the student.

## Brand Personality

Formal, institutional, and precise. The product should communicate academic reliability and procedural clarity without feeling bureaucratic or difficult to use.

## Anti-references

- Playful consumer apps with gamification, decorative illustrations, or casual visual language.
- Dense admin dashboards dominated by sidebars, metric cards, and unrelated system information.

## Design Principles

- Ask for the student's courses before requesting screenshots.
- Keep every screenshot visibly associated with its course and permit multiple screenshots per course.
- Never introduce a course, section, or meeting that the student did not supply.
- Make schedule validity and bundled course components easy to inspect.
- Keep the workflow sequential and focused: courses, screenshots, verification, then schedules.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, persistent labels, readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, practical touch targets, and responsive layouts. Important statuses and schedule distinctions must be communicated with text rather than color alone.
